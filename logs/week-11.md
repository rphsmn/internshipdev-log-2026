### 💡 Key Learnings

* **Workflow Orchestration:** Formalized the "Final Approval" rule where credits are only deducted upon HR’s terminal authorization. This prevents double-deductions and ensures balance accuracy across the lifecycle of a leave request.
* **Automated Credit Intelligence:** Documented and verified the system’s ability to handle **tenure-based credit increases** and automated calculations for holidays and weekends, ensuring the software dynamically scales as an employee grows with the company.
* **Security & Privacy Architecture:** Hardened data privacy settings to ensure **Government IDs** and other sensitive identifiers are strictly limited to the HR department and the individual owner, preventing unauthorized access across the directory.
* **Operational Reporting:** Integrated a reporting module that allows HR to export formatted leave reports and manually track "no-show" absences for comprehensive personnel management.
